# RAMP is a thinking route for every practical

<div class="ramp-route">
  <div><span>R</span><strong>Recognise hazards</strong><p>What has the potential to cause harm?</p></div>
  <div><span>A</span><strong>Assess risks</strong><p>How likely and how serious is harm in this method?</p></div>
  <div><span>M</span><strong>Minimise risks</strong><p>Which safer method, equipment and behaviour reduce exposure?</p></div>
  <div><span>P</span><strong>Prepare for emergencies</strong><p>What will we do if the controls fail?</p></div>
</div>

<p class="ramp-refrain">RAMP happens before, during and after the practical—not only after an accident.</p>

<div class="hazard-field">

# Strong alkalis can keep moving deeper into eye tissue

<p class="base-note">A <strong>base</strong> reacts with an acid. An <strong>alkali</strong> is a base that dissolves in water.</p>

<figure class="eye-comparison-figure">
  <img :src="'images/acid-alkali-eye-comparison-v2.png'" alt="Simplified comparison of acid surface damage and deeper alkali penetration through the front layers of the eye" />
</figure>

<div class="corrosive-comparison">
  <p><span>Many acids</span><strong>make surface proteins coagulate, forming a barrier that can slow penetration.</strong></p>
  <p><span>Strong alkalis</span><strong>disrupt cell membranes and can penetrate the cornea rapidly.</strong></p>
</div>

<p class="corrosive-warning">Both can cause permanent injury. Chemical, concentration, amount and contact time all matter.</p>

# Observation, measurement and inference do different jobs

<div class="omi-comparison">
  <div><span>Observation</span><strong>What do I directly notice?</strong><p>“The liquid is cloudy.”</p></div>
  <div><span>Measurement</span><strong>What quantity did an instrument show?</strong><p>“The liquid is 18.4 °C.”</p></div>
  <div><span>Inference</span><strong>What might the evidence mean?</strong><p>“A new solid may have formed.”</p></div>
</div>

<p class="omi-warning">An inference can be reasonable without being directly seen.</p>

# Sensible precision stops where the instrument stops

# Simple calculations should keep their units visible

<div class="worked-composition">
  <div class="worked-case">
    <span>Mass by difference</span>
    <p>container + sample = <b>58.2 g</b></p>
    <p>empty container = <b>32.0 g</b></p>

  </div>
  <div class="worked-case">

  </div>
</div>
